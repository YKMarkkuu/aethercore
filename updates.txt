🚨 TIER 1: Legal / Safety Essentials (Build These First)

These are the ones that actually matter for liability, especially under Philippine DPA and general platform law.
1. Report System ⚠️ URGENT

Users must be able to report:

    Posts

    Comments

    Users (profiles)

    Messages (DMs)

    Spaces / Space messages

Why: Any platform with UGC needs a report mechanism. Without it, you have no way to know what's happening, and no defense if a user sues for "you allowed this."

Minimum: A "Report" button + reports table + basic admin review list.
2. Block System ⚠️ URGENT

Users must be able to block other users. When blocked:

    Their posts don't show in your feed

    Their messages don't reach you

    They can't see your profile (or see a limited version)

    They can't send you friend requests

    They can't mention/notify you

Why: This is a safety feature, not a "nice to have." If a user is being harassed and can't block the harasser, that's a real problem — legally and ethically.

Minimum: A blocks table + filter in feed/DMs/profile queries.
3. Admin Dashboard (Minimal) ⚠️ URGENT

Without it, you can't actually use the report or block data. You need at minimum:

    A page listing reports

    One-click delete post/comment

    One-click ban/suspend user

    User search

Why: A report system without a way to action reports is useless. This is the piece that ties it together.
4. Terms of Service — Actually Enforceable ⚠️ REVIEW

Your current ToS exists, but does it clearly state:

    What content is prohibited (harassment, CSAM, doxxing, etc.)

    Your right to remove content and ban users

    Your DMCA/takedown process

    User responsibility for their content

Why: If a user posts illegal content, you need a ToS that says "we can remove this and ban you." Without it, you have less legal cover.
5. Age Restriction / Verification ⚠️ CONSIDER

Most platforms require 13+ (COPPA) or 18+ for certain features. If a minor joins and something bad happens, you could be liable.

Minimum: A checkbox on signup: "I confirm I am 13+ years old."
🎯 TIER 2: Strongly Recommended (Build Right After)
6. Content Moderation Tools

    Auto-flag content with certain keywords

    Ability to soft-delete (hide from public but keep in DB for legal records)

    Edit user content (rare, but sometimes needed)

7. Account Suspension System

Not just "ban/unban" — you'll want:

    Temporary suspension (7 days, 30 days)

    Reason attached to the ban

    User-visible ban notice when they try to log in

8. Privacy Controls

    Private account toggle (only friends see posts)

    Who can DM me? (Everyone / Friends only / Nobody)

    Who can see my Spaces?

9. Data Export / Deletion (DPA Requirement)

Under DPA, users have the right to access and delete their data. You need:

    "Download my data" button

    "Delete my account" (you have this — make sure it's complete)

10. Session / Device Management

See where you're logged in. Log out other sessions. This prevents account takeover.
🎯 TIER 3: Important but Not Urgent
11. Email Verification

Prevents fake accounts and improves safety.
12. Rate Limiting

Prevents spam floods. Laravel has this built-in — just apply it to post/comment/message routes.
13. 2FA (Optional)

For high-value accounts, but not required for MVP.
14. Content Warning / NSFW Flags

Optional, but useful for user-generated content.
